Meet Mitch

Mitch began Wildrose Carpet Cleaning in 2023, a family-owned and operated truck mounted carpet cleaning service. WCC has since moved to Spruce Grove, where Mitch grew up.

Since 2023, Wildrose has grown into a full-service janitorial and carpet cleaning operation serving as south as Red Deer and as north as Yellowknife.

Despite the scale of our janitorial work, Mitch still enjoys local repair and restretch jobs. It keeps him hands-on and working with what he loves most; carpet!
